# Medical Policy Applied Research Team (MPART)
### Office of Medicaid Innovation | University of Illinois Springfield

Welcome to the official GitHub organization for the **Medical Policy Applied Research Team (MPART)**. We operate at the intersection of the University of Illinois Springfield and the Office of Medicaid Innovation, focusing on the development of high-integrity research methodologies.

---

## 👋 Community & Collaboration
This space is dedicated to sharing tools, templates, and frameworks with the broader academic and policy research community. We believe that as AI changes the landscape of qualitative inquiry, the need for open, reproducible, and "human-in-the-loop" standards is paramount.

---

## 🛠️ Featured Tool

### [vibe_research](https://github.com/illinoismpart/vibe_research)
Our starter template for academics. It bridges the gap between the agility of "vibe coding" and the rigor of academic inquiry.

* **Tiered Integrity:** Switch between **Research Mode** for discovery and **Compliance Mode** for a GPG-signed, 100% cited audit trail.
* **Provenance First:** Every claim is structurally bound to a source via SHA256 digital fingerprints.
* **Built for Humans:** Absorbs the "extraneous load" of data management so researchers can focus on "germane" policy analysis.



---

## ⚖️ Scope & Responsibility
The tools hosted here are designed to improve **traceability and citation discipline**. While our frameworks enforce structural integrity and document provenance, they do not replace the necessity for professional judgment, regulatory expertise, or formal peer review. Analytical validity and interpretive correctness remain the sole responsibility of the human researcher.
